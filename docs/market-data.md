# Market data

The market module is not connected in the current Phase 0–2 milestone. It shows an honest not-configured state.

Future records must include provider, observation timestamp, currency, exchange, delay, adjustment status, and license/attribution. Equities and ETFs are distinct asset classes; ETFs must never be labeled derivatives. Any AI Exposure Score is inferred until reviewed and must expose its inputs and limitations. No exact price, return, or performance value may appear without a verified source record.
